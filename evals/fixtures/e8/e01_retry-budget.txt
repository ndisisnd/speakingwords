So the thing to understand here is that the retry budget is currently set to three attempts in total. After those three attempts have all been used up, what actually happens is that the job gets moved across into the dead-letter queue. In other words, a job that fails four separate times will stop retrying altogether and will simply sit there waiting for a human being to come along and take a look at it. The parked jobs themselves live in `queue/dead`, and the `bin/requeue` script is the thing that puts them back.
