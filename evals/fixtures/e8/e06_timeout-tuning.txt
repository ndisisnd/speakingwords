I had a look at the configuration and there are a few things going on. The HTTP timeout is currently set to 5 seconds, and the p99 latency on the upstream call that it is wrapping is around 8 seconds. That mismatch is what is producing the errors that you are seeing in the logs. The change that I would suggest is raising the timeout to 15 seconds in `config/http.json`, which gives a comfortable margin.
