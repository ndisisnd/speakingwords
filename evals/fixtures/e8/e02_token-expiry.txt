It is probably worth explaining how the session token works before getting into the specifics. The token expires 15 minutes after the moment it is first issued, and the refresh call really does have to land before that deadline passes. To put it another way, a client that goes to sleep for 20 minutes is always going to come back to a token that is already dead. The refresh logic itself lives in `lib/session.js:88` if you want to read through it.
